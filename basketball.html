Basketball — Drills, pickup games, and tips

Improve shooting, dribbling and court awareness.

Overview

Basketball is a fast, high-skill sport. Work on ball handling and conditioning to excel.

Quick tips

  • Practice shooting form close to the basket before moving back.
  • Use cone drills to improve dribbling under pressure.
  • Play pickup games to develop decision-making in live play.

Local play

Community courts often host pickup games — check turfs and local groups.
