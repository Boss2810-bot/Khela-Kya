Cricket — Fast facts & how to get started

Everything you need to know about playing and watching cricket.

Overview

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of eleven players. It has multiple formats — Test, ODI, and T20 — each with a different pace and strategy.

Quick tips

  • Work on timing and footwork; most good batting is timing over raw power.
  • Bowlers focus on line and length; practise control before speed.
  • Fielding drills can save matches — agility and awareness matter.
