Tennis — Basics, drills and where to practice

Serve, rally, and improve with practical drills.

Overview

Tennis is played in singles or doubles. Focus on consistency and placement to win points.

Quick tips

  • Practice serves to gain free points; target placement over speed.
  • Work on footwork drills to reach wide balls efficiently.
  • Rally with a partner focusing on cross-court consistency.

Local play

Beginner lessons and court rentals are commonly available — check turfs for locations.
