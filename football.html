Football — Key facts & how to join pickup games

Learn about leagues, tips for beginners, and where to play.

Overview

Football (soccer) is played by two teams of eleven. It's the world's most popular sport, with tactical depth and global competitions.

Quick tips

  • Focus on passing and first touch; control spreads play.
  • Improve stamina with interval training for repeated sprints.
  • Small-sided games (5v5) emphasize skill and decision-making.
